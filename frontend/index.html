4-Step Clarity Flow

A gentle, optional reflection exercise—not a clinical assessment. Skip any step you don't want to answer.

Step 1 of 4 • Understand the Situation

What happened, or what is weighing on you right now?

Just the simple facts—no need to write it perfectly.

Step 2 of 4 • Thoughts & Feelings

What recurring thoughts or emotions are showing up?

Putting them into words helps separate what you feel from what is a proven fact.

Step 3 of 4 • Alternative Perspective

Without dismissing your feelings, what else might be true?

Your feelings are valid. Is there another angle or a strength you've used before?

Step 4 of 4 • One Realistic Next Step

What is one small, realistic step you could try next?

Even a 10-minute action or taking a short break counts.

Privacy & Safety

Clear, honest details about your data and verified support resources.

🔒 Your Data & Controls

  • • No account required: You can use AUREVIA without signing up.
  • • Local device storage: Your moods, habits, and saved chat list are stored in your browser's localStorage.
  • • AI processing: When you send a chat message, it is sent over HTTPS to our backend server to generate a reply. Do not share passwords, government IDs, or financial details.
  • • Deletion: Deleting a chat removes it from your browser immediately. Server/provider-side temporary logs depend on the AI provider's policy.

🚨 Urgent Support & Helplines

AUREVIA cannot contact emergency services or monitor you in real time. If you or someone you know is in immediate danger or distress, please reach out to a trusted person or a verified helpline:

• India Emergency Services: 112

• Tele-MANAS (Govt. of India, 24/7 Free): 14416 / 1800-891-4416

• KIRAN Mental Health Helpline: 1800-599-0019

• International Helplines: findahelpline.com

How AUREVIA Works

Transparent information about our approach, capabilities, and limitations.

🧭 What AUREVIA Is (and Isn't)

  • • Self-Reflection Tool: Helps you organize thoughts, practice grounding, and explore calmer perspectives.
  • • Evidence-Informed Concepts: Inspired by general CBT reframing, reflective questioning, and mindfulness exercises.
  • • Not Medical Care: Does not diagnose conditions, provide psychotherapy, or replace licensed professionals or trusted human relationships.

⚙️ AI & Limitations

  • • AI-Generated Responses: Replies are generated by an AI language model via our backend API and may occasionally be inaccurate or miss context.
  • • Offline Tools: Breathe & Ground, Mood Check-In (local save), Daily Habits, and Frequency Audio run directly in your browser.
  • • Safety Guardrails: Built-in crisis detection pauses ordinary advice and surfaces human support resources if distress is detected.
