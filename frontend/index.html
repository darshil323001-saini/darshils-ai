Privacy & Safety

Your safety and data privacy are our top priorities.

🔒 Your Privacy

  • • Anonymous by default: No registration, no account, no sign-in required.
  • • Local storage only: Mood logs, reflections, and habits are stored in YOUR browser, never on a server.
  • • No tracking: No analytics, no cookies, no third-party data collection.
  • • Your control: Delete all your data anytime with one click from the tools themselves.
  • • Conversations not stored server-side: Chat sessions exist only in RAM while the backend runs. Restarting the server clears all conversations.
  • • No data sharing: Nothing is shared with any third party — ever.

⚠️ Important Disclaimer

  • • DARSHIL'S AI is an informational wellness companion, not a licensed therapist, psychologist, psychiatrist, or medical professional.
  • • It does not provide clinical diagnoses or prescribe medication.
  • • It is not a substitute for professional mental health care.
  • • If you are experiencing a mental health crisis, please contact a qualified professional immediately.
